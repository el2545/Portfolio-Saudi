03 — AI Decision Engine

The AI Decision Engine: Auditable Smart City Capital Allocation.

Transitioning from predictive forecasting to normative strategy. This engine integrates XGBoost demand predictions with carbon-adjusted Decoupled Net Present Value (DNPV) and AROMAN ranking. It is built to prioritize Vision 2030 infrastructure investments under severe geoeconomic and climate uncertainty, proving that multi-criteria scorecards offer superior auditability over single-indicator NPV models.

Layer 1 · Macro-Environmental Risk

Energy scenarios, EU ETS carbon-cost exposure (€0 to €150/tCO₂), and Geopolitical Risk (GPR) indices.

Layer 2 · AI Prediction

XGBoost demand forecasting. Proof of capability: 45.8% Mean Absolute Error (MAE) reduction compared to Ridge regression baselines.

Layer 3 · Valuation & Weighting

Carbon-adjusted DNPV and Real Options Valuation combined with hybrid Fuzzy-AHP/MEREC weighting.

Layer 4 · AROMAN Ranking

Cross-validated against TOPSIS and COPRAS. AROMAN diverged from NPV-only rankings across all 25 tested projects.
