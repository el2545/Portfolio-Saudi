A Vibrant Society / My Story

The Seven Saints Regraga: an ancestral departure toward Mecca

According to Regraga oral tradition, seven Amazigh saints journeyed from Morocco to Mecca before the Hijra, met the Prophet Muhammad, and were entrusted with carrying Islam back to the Maghreb. The tradition preserves the beautiful idea that they were welcomed in their own Berber language — a symbol of faith, transmission, and dignity.

Presented respectfully as an ancestral narrative and cultural memory.

A Thriving Economy / Commercialization

From academic model to SME-ready decision tool: a clear commercialization path.

The scoring model has a clear commercialization path aimed at making complex infrastructure investment analysis accessible to SMEs, municipalities, and development banks — all within Saudi Arabia's economic diversification agenda.

Phase 1 — SaaS prototype

Build a lightweight scoring dashboard allowing users to input asset parameters and receive a multi-criteria investment recommendation.

Phase 2 — Validation partnership

Partner with a Saudi municipality or public investment body to test the model against real infrastructure decisions and refine coefficients.

Phase 3 — SME toolkit

Package the model as an accessible digital tool for SMEs bidding on public infrastructure contracts — reducing information asymmetry.

Digital Ethics / Al-Amānah

Honest AI: why I refuse to present this portfolio as more than it is.

Every claim in this portfolio is supported by documented evidence, clearly separated from proposed research directions. Academic honesty is treated as a faith commitment.

The GIFT framework: honesty built into the structure

Every research element in this portfolio is tagged with its evidence type: G (Gap identified), I (Idea proposed), F (Feasibility documented), T (Timeline planned). No claim presents proposed work as completed achievement.

Al-Amānah as a research standard

Al-Amānah (الأمانة, trustworthiness) is one of the Prophet's four core attributes. In a research context, it means: every number is sourced, every assumption is declared, every model limitation is acknowledged. This is how I approach academic work.

No phantom results

Results from the Casablanca PFE are the only documented quantitative outputs. All Saudi-facing extensions are clearly framed as proposed research.

Source citations visible

Vision 2030 statistics referenced in this portfolio are attributed to their original sources: Progress Report 2023, NTP 2020-2024, PIF Annual Report 2022.

Explainable models only

No black-box outputs. Every scoring element has a named variable, documented formula, and traceable coefficient.

Cultural claims are qualified

The Regraga narrative is presented as oral tradition and cultural memory — never as historical fact. The NEOM Line is a conceptual metaphor, not a personal connection.

Research Manifesto

Five principles guiding my academic practice in Saudi Arabia.

Tawaḍuʿ

Intellectual humility

I begin every research question by assuming I do not yet have the answer. Models are tools for learning, not instruments for confirmation.

ʿAdl

Equity in method

A scoring model that only works for large corporates is not a good model. Every output I design should be accessible to SMEs and public institutions.

Iḥsān

Excellence in execution

Every dataset, every assumption, every visualization must be the best I am capable of. Not good enough — excellent.

Amānah

Trustworthiness

Transparent sourcing, declared limitations, and reproducible methods. Research that cannot be checked is not research worth doing.

Khidmah

Service orientation

The ultimate test of any research contribution is whether it genuinely helps a decision-maker make a better choice for their community.
