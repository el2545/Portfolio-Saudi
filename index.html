A Vibrant Society / My Story

From the Seven Saints Regraga to a modern academic journey toward the heart of the Islamic world.

The Regraga journey is presented as cultural memory and personal frame — not a historical proof claim.

The Seven Saints Regraga: an ancestral departure toward Mecca

According to Regraga oral tradition, seven Amazigh saints journeyed from Morocco to Mecca before the Hijra, met the Prophet Muhammad, and were entrusted with carrying Islam back to the Maghreb. The tradition also preserves the beautiful idea that they were welcomed in their own Berber language, making the journey a symbol of faith, transmission, and dignity.

Presented respectfully as an ancestral narrative and cultural memory.

My move toward Saudi Arabia is therefore not presented as a simple study plan. Digital Ethics

Trustworthy analytics for public-value research.

The digital ethics statement is visible, not buried in a footer, because scholarship reviewers should see technical ambition balanced with governance maturity.

Data minimization

Collect only variables required for the research question; prefer aggregated and anonymized indicators.

Explainability

Every score must be traceable to clear criteria: cost, service coverage, risk, sustainability, or socioeconomic relevance.

Human oversight

The model supports expert judgment; it does not replace governance, academic supervision, or stakeholder review.

No surveillance frame

Mobility and urban data should serve planning and public value, never intrusive tracking or discriminatory profiling.

Evidence Check / Implementation Realism

Vision 2030 is treated as an active transformation, not as a completed success.

This portfolio uses Vision 2030 as an alignment frame. It separates stable official structure, sector priorities, proposed research contribution, and implementation limits.

Stable foundation

The three pillars, diversification away from oil, non-oil sector growth, tourism, technology, renewables, and SME development are treated as stable Vision 2030 themes.

Portfolio rule: use these as strategic context, not personal achievements.

Sectors still in progress

Healthcare transformation, education reform, human capability development, digital health, PPPs, sustainability, and renewable infrastructure are presented as ongoing national programs with measurable progress and remaining challenges.

Portfolio rule: write target, reform, pathway, or program; avoid claiming completion.

Critical realism

The portfolio acknowledges that implementation capacity, governance, human capital, and social/political constraints can affect delivery. This makes the research tone more credible and less promotional.

Portfolio rule: ambition plus verification, never blind celebration.
